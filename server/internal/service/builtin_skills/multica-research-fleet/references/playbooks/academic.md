# Academic research playbook

Use after `general.md` when the decision question requires academic evidence.

## Evidence adaptation

- Match study design, population, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome,
  and time boundary to the Claim.
- Peer review and venue are provenance signals, not proof. Inspect methods,
  sample size, effect size, uncertainty, limitations, conflicts of interest,
  retractions/corrections, and replication evidence when material.
- Preprints, datasets, lab material, proceedings, and reproducibility reports
  can be fit evidence when their role and review status are explicit.

## Method adaptation

- Use systematic-search protocols, FINER, IRB, PRISMA, CONSORT, STROBE, or a
  citation style only when the Research Contract or chosen study design calls
  for them.
- Define search databases, query families, screening rules, date bounds, and
  quality appraisal before collection when completeness claims are made.
- Treat publication bias, conflicting results, and replication gaps as
  counterevidence; preserve them in Claims and Evidence Links.
