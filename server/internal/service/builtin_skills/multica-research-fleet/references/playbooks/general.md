# General research method playbook

Use this before any domain adaptation. The accepted `run.method` remains
authoritative when a playbook example differs from it.

## Frame the decision

- State the decision question, unit of analysis, comparison boundary, time
  range, geography, audience, and output use.
- Split required questions by what would change the answer, not by generic
  topic headings.
- Record assumptions and what evidence would weaken or reverse the current
  answer.

## Select methods by question

- Choice between options: constraint-based comparison plus sensitivity and
  failure analysis.
- Size, rate, or trend: measurement definition, denominator, time series, and
  uncertainty analysis.
- Why something happens: competing mechanisms, process tracing, and negative
  cases.
- What happened: chronology, source provenance, and contradiction resolution.
- Risk or due diligence: exposure, likelihood, consequence, controls, and
  disconfirming cases.
- Broad discovery: exploratory mapping followed by a versioned replan with
  narrower questions and explicit evidence requirements.

## Judge evidence fitness

- Match each source to the Claim it can establish. A vendor page can establish
  its published price; it cannot independently establish comparative quality.
- Preserve source independence, provenance, date, scope, and exact
  observations. Do not use a universal source hierarchy as a substitute for
  this judgment.
- Use counter-search for material claims and decision-reversing assumptions.

## Stop and report

- Stop only when required questions meet their evidence requirements,
  material counterevidence is resolved or explicit, and another bounded search
  has negligible expected information gain.
- Report the applied method, evidence, contrary findings, uncertainty,
  limitations, unresolved gaps, and decision consequence.
