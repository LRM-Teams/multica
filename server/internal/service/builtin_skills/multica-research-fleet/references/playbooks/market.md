# Market research playbook

Use after `general.md` for market, competitor, customer, pricing, or go-to-market
questions.

- Define market, customer, unit, geography, currency, time, and denominator
  before comparing size, growth, price, or share.
- Company filings and product/pricing pages establish reported facts and vendor
  positioning. Customer behavior, comparative quality, adoption, and market
  impact require independent or directly observed evidence.
- Reviews, forums, job postings, rankings, analyst estimates, and search trends
  are signals with selection and measurement bias; state what each can and
  cannot establish.

## Counterevidence adaptation

- Search for churn, failed launches, hidden price conditions, non-adoption,
  channel conflict, regulatory constraints, and estimates with incompatible
  definitions.
- Reconcile conflicting market numbers by definition and calculation method;
  do not average incompatible estimates.
